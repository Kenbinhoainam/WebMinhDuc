An example role for organized team players who want to develop in logistics.

  • Logistics
  • Teamwork

Job description

  • Receive, count and place goods in the correct areas.
  • Pick and pack goods and verify delivery information.
  • Keep the warehouse tidy and report any discrepancies.

What makes you a good fit?

  • Able to count accurately and cooperate with colleagues.
  • Follow warehouse safety rules.
  • Willing to learn warehouse equipment and forms.

Benefits and support

  • Training in goods receiving and dispatch procedures.
  • Team environment with guidance during the initial period.
  • Benefits are confirmed during consultation about an actual position.
