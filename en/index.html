About Minh Đức Vina

Minh Đức connects factories in northern Vietnam with workers, from clarifying the role to bringing both sides together to discuss the job.

Employers can discuss shift staffing, skilled recruitment and management processes. We also help jobseekers and students explore opportunities that fit their needs.
