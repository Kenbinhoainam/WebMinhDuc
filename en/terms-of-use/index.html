Using the Minh Đức website

This content describes the scope of the prototype experience and must be reviewed by Minh Đức before the official website launches.

1. Prototype scope

This website is for experiencing and reviewing the interface, job-search journeys, service information and request flows. Content labeled illustrative is not an official job posting or event announcement.

2. Recruitment and service information

Confirm recruitment conditions, pay, service fees and the scope of cooperation directly with Minh Đức or the recruiting organization before making a decision.

3. Using the forms

Users should provide accurate information appropriate to the form’s purpose. Submission in this prototype is simulated and does not create a real application, advice request or event registration.

4. Documents and images

The design follows references supplied by the user. Illustrative images must be reviewed and replaced with approved assets before use on the official website.

5. External links and content

The website may link to external contact and information platforms. Their content may change; verify accounts and contacts before sharing personal information.
