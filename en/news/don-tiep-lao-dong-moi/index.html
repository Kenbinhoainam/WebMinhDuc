Preparing a new worker’s first day

From appointments to the person providing guidance, small details help the first day run smoothly.

  1. Before the start date
  2. On the first day
  3. After work begins

Before the start date

Send clear instructions on the address, entrance, arrival time and required documents. Confirm a contact workers can reach for support.

On the first day

Take time to explain duties, rules and essential safety guidance. Check that workers understand instead of simply handing over documents.

  • Introduce the direct manager or mentor.
  • Show work areas and shared facilities.
  • Explain attendance, leave reporting and question procedures.
  • Record any questions that arise.

After work begins

A brief check-in helps identify early difficulties. Feedback can improve onboarding for future groups.
