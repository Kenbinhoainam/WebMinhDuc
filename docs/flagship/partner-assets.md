# Partner marks and source

The five names come from the user-provided [Minh Đức information sheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1SFbcwntvl7E13yGO--6tAISyIuxddhx4UwsrOGgx6VA/edit), INDEX rows 20–24. The sheet provides names, not logo files or proof of any additional affiliation.

- **Samsung** uses the black SAMSUNG lettermark embedded as an SVG in the footer of Samsung's official Global Newsroom. The local copy preserves the original path and fill. [Samsung Global Newsroom](https://news.samsung.com/global/) · [Official color and clear-space guidance](https://www.samsung.com/uk/about-us/brand-identity/logo/)
- **Foxconn** uses the official red and white Hon Hai Technology Group / Foxconn lockup from the corporate header. The local SVG is the original file and appears against a dark surface for contrast. [Corporate website](https://www.foxconn.com/en-us) · [Original SVG](https://image.honhai.com/upload/global/logo-en-us_202311301025578760108.svg)
- **Luxshare ICT** uses the official blue and green corporate lockup from the English homepage. The local SVG preserves the original vector and colors. [Corporate website](https://www.luxshare-ict.com/en/) · [Original SVG](https://www.luxshare-ict.com/Public/Uploads/uploadfile/images/20231018/logo2.svg)
- **JEILTECH VINA** is shown under the GLOVITECH corporate mark, with its Vietnamese subsidiary name printed underneath. GLOVITECH records the establishment of JEIL TECH VINA in 2015 and the Korean company's subsequent rename in 2020. Its Vietnam operations profile identifies the Vân Trung plant. The local lossless WebP preserves the official parent-company artwork. [Corporate history](https://glovitech.co.kr/company/history.php) · [Operations](https://glovitech.co.kr/en/company/info.php) · [Vietnam factory profile](https://vn.emidas.com/en/company/detail/4007) · [Original PNG](https://glovitech.co.kr/img/top_logo.png)
- **TACU** uses the corporate mark on TACU Group’s contact page, which lists TACU TECHNOLOGY (Vietnam) CO., LTD. in Vân Trung. The asset is stored locally as lossless WebP. [Official group contact page](https://www.tacuap.com/contact/show.php?id=92&lang=en) · [Original PNG](https://www.tacuap.com/upload/202202/1644639601.png)

All artwork is local and the SVGs contain no scripts or external references. Marks remain the property of their respective owners; their inclusion represents the names in the supplied list and makes no further claim about a relationship. The project owner confirmed permission to display the listed marks.
