# Partner marks and source

The five names come from the user-provided [Minh Đức information sheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1SFbcwntvl7E13yGO--6tAISyIuxddhx4UwsrOGgx6VA/edit), INDEX rows 20–24. The sheet provides names, not logo files or proof of any additional affiliation.

- **Samsung:** local `public/partners/samsung.svg` is the path from [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/icons/samsung.svg) (blob `627cbf2c117a399bd966bbaced725369bd5b392f`), a third-party vector. The viewBox was tightened and the fill set to Samsung Blue, as specified on [Samsung's identity page](https://www.samsung.com/us/about-us/brand-identity/logo/). The underlying path is unchanged.
- **Foxconn:** local `public/partners/foxconn.svg` comes from [cascade-money's copy](https://github.com/mcorrig4/cascade-money/blob/5de49fa24665a0d162ed0b66913a594181d3979f/app/public/logos/foxconn.svg), attributed there to [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Foxconn_logo.svg). It is a third-party vector, not a download from Foxconn. [Foxconn's company profile](https://www.foxconn.com/us/about/group-profile) identifies Hon Hai Technology Group as Foxconn.
- **Luxshare ICT:** local `public/partners/luxshare.webp` is an optimized copy of [this third-party PNG](https://github.com/htferretti/liberaopreto/blob/80b7f8d0c897190711099dc9822d306386a13a12/src/assets/manufactors/luxshare.png), blob `422ccb6b54178f06aa296bedbf0d5858b025423f`. Its blue and green mark matches the mark shown in the [official Luxshare report](https://www.luxshare-ict.com/Public/Uploads/uploadfile/files/20231101/2021E.pdf). The source is 418 × 140 pixels; the local copy was converted to WebP with metadata removed.
- **Jeiltech Vina and TACU:** plain text. The supplied sheet does not uniquely establish an official logo for either name; similarly named organizations were not substituted.

All artwork is local and the SVGs contain no scripts or external references. Marks remain the property of their respective owners; their inclusion represents the names in the supplied list and makes no further claim about a relationship. Confirm permission and current branding before production.
